# BabyNews (베이비뉴스) MCP

Read-only tools over BabyNews (ibabynews.com), a Korean news outlet on pregnancy, childbirth and parenting since 2010.

- Articles: search, latest, by category, related, single article (first 2,000 characters plus the original URL).
- Q&A: parenting, wedding, pets, insurance, law and labor, based on official sources.
- Regional childbirth and parenting support programs by city and district in Korea.

When using results, cite the article title, publication date and original URL. Health, medical and legal content is for reference only.
Guide: https://www.ibabynews.com/mcp-guide · Privacy: https://www.ibabynews.com/com/privacy.html
